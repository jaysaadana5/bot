Frequently asked

Quick answers for busy founders.

Who do you typically partner with?

Seed to Series B developer-tool, AI infrastructure, and open-source companies. Teams of 10 – 80 with a product in market and a founder committed to distribution.

How are engagements structured?

We work on a monthly retainer with a 3-month minimum commitment and a fixed scope per quarter. Equity-light partnerships are available for exceptional early-stage teams.

What's a typical investment?

Single-service engagements start at $8k/month. Embedded growth-team engagements range $20–45k/month depending on scope, cadence, and team size. We scope transparently after discovery.

Do you work with non-developer brands?

Rarely. Our edge is dev-first context — protocols, SDKs, open source, AI infra. If your audience isn't developers, we'll happily refer you to studios who'd serve you better.

How do you measure success?

Every engagement defines 3 – 5 leading KPIs up front (e.g. qualified signups, community growth, share of voice, activation rate) with a shared dashboard updated weekly.

Can you replace an in-house DevRel hire?

Short-term, yes. Long-term we aim to make ourselves replaceable — helping you hire, onboard, and train your in-house team so systems survive the handoff.
